How I work: five principles

01 From problem to production

I’m a product-minded engineer who doesn't just ship code. I like to start with a customer pain point, bridge knowledge across teams and ship a working solution. Then, I stick with it until it runs smoothly in the real world. Bring in the problem, I'll handle the follow-through.

02 Better done than perfect

I prioritize working and reliable solutions over sophisticated ones. They’re not fancy, sure, but they create momentum. Shipping early and often creates real value. Building is thinking, and your users are your best beta testers. We build with them, not just for them.

03 Deep dive and deliver

Sometimes, good enough ain’t good enough. The problem is complex, the bug ambiguous. That’s when I switch into research mode: I dig into docs, test edge cases, and compare approaches. Every task is a learning opportunity for the whole team, so you’re not just getting code, you’re getting a clear path forward, with a rationale and the next steps ready.

04 Great communication, not just technical solutions

I’m fluent in both XML and ROI. I’ll communicate clearly with you, whether you’re technical or not, so you always know what we’re solving, why it matters, and how it’ll help your business.

05 You know your business

Let’s be real, you know your customers better than I do. Together, we partner to turn your knowledge into tech that’ll make your business move forward. One problem at a time.
