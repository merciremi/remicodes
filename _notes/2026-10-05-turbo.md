Today I learned a nifty Turbo Frames trick: how to persist scroll depth accross a page refresh.

I'm currently working on bundles for cults3D. On the general index of bundles, I have a section displaying 6 bundles manually selected by the Cults' team. Then, I have my list of bundles sorted by newest to latest. 

<!-- screencap -->

I'm working on adding a component with some filters in it: toggles, sorting, you know, the work. And this poses a problem: when I toggle a filter, the whole page reloads and I'm back at the top of the page. So to see my filtered bundles, I have to re-scroll through the selected-bundles section, filters, etc... It's frustating.

Since we're using Hotwire as a progressive enhancement tech, I used that.

I wrapped my filters component and bundles list in turbo frame tags with three nifty attributes:
- `autoscroll: true`
- `data-autoscroll-block="start"`
- `turbo_action: :advance`

## `autoscroll: true`

After the frame’s content is swapped, Turbo scrolls the frame into view. This only happens on frame navigations, not on a first visit or reload.

## `data-autoscroll-block="start"`

This attribute makes the start of the frame to align with the top of the screen.

## `turbo_action: :advance` 

This attribute turns a frame navigation to a "full" page visit.

It updates the URL (in my case, with my filters parameters – ie `myurl.com?no_ai=true`) and adds a history entry on each frame navigation. 

Check the [documentation here](https://turbo.hotwired.dev/handbook/frames#promoting-a-frame-navigation-to-a-page-visit).

The results are really cool!
