date: 2026-11-12 14:46:07 +0100

In a recent conversation on Reddit, a user raised doubts about the quality of [the Ruby Stained-Glass Panel](/projects/ruby-stained-glass/) based on the pictures of the prototype.

I've had a lot of thought about their remarks but the main takeaway I have right now is:

In a world full of grifters, you have to go the extra 100 miles to prove you're genuinely invested in making something truly beautiful. People can't assume that without getting to know you more than in passing.
